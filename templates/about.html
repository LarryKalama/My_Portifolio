I have practical experience applying technology to solve business and operational challenges through enterprise ICT support, infrastructure management, software development, and data-driven solutions

The system combines business record management, performance reporting, artificial intelligence, and communication integrations in one platform.

Key Features:

  • Reports and insights for business planning and decision-making.

Technical Focus: Python, Django, database-driven web development, AI integration, API integration, business analytics, and mobile payment systems.

Key Features:

The Cafeteria Management System is a web-based application designed to streamline cafeteria operations through digital order processing, sales management, and transaction tracking.   • Tools for managing daily cafeteria operations.

Technical Focus: Python, Django, database management, POS development, payment API integration, and role-based access control.
